Hi, I'm Lucy

I'm an indie developer and 3D artist who works in Blender, Godot, and Unity.

I specialize in clean, low-poly environments designed for beginners and rapid prototyping.

I enjoy creating narrative-driven projects in my free time.

AI Use Policy

i do not consent having my art be used for AI training purposes

I use AI as a programming assistant, not for “vibe coding.” I rely on it because traditional search engines have become unreliable and time-consuming to use. more info

AI helps me work through more complex programming challenges that would otherwise take significantly longer to solve through searching alone.

Programming assistance is used only for solo game projects and for internal tools I have developed to improve the efficiency of asset creation workflows.

These represent a small portion of the overall development process. I hold an Advanced Diploma in Game Design and have been programming for many years.

While AI may be part of the process in a limited capacity, it is used solely to improve productivity and not as a replacement for technical knowledge or creative decision-making.

For Unity projects, i add character controller as a extra it was created by me prior to the use of AI in context.

I will never use generative AI in any of my asset packs. I believe doing so would be dishonest and unethical, and I do not agree with how AI-generated art is created.

If you purchase anything from me, you are guaranteed that all art and textures are 100% human-made.and intentionally crafted
